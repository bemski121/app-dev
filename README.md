# My Favorite Series

## Breaking Bad

**Genre:** Crime, Drama, Thriller

**Main Character:** Walter White

### About the Series
Breaking Bad is a television series about a chemistry teacher named Walter White who starts making illegal drugs after discovering that he has cancer.

### Why I Like This Series
I like this series because:
- It has an interesting storyline.
- The characters are very entertaining.
- Every episode is exciting.
- It shows how decisions can change someone's life.

### My Favorite Character
**Jesse Pinkman** - He is an interesting character who experiences many challenges throughout the series.
